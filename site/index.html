Set it up

For Mac and Windows · iPhone

Left a file on your laptop? Get it on your phone.

Remote Desk lets your iPhone find, preview and save the files on your own laptop, from anywhere: mobile data, a café, another city. It only reads, it works with one phone, and your files travel through your own encrypted network.

  • Read-only
  • Paired to one phone
  • No iCloud plan needed
  • $0 for personal use

Why pay for cloud storage?

Bye-bye, iCloud storage plan.

Your files already live on your laptop. Remote Desk lets your iPhone open them from anywhere, so you don't have to upload everything to iCloud, or keep paying for more space, just to have your files with you.

Set it up for free

Still like iCloud for photos and iPhone backups? Keep it. You just don't need it to reach the files on your laptop.

Everything on your laptop, a tap away

Open the Remote Desk app on your Home Screen, enter your PIN, and your laptop's folders are there.

  • Browse your folders

    Your home folder, or just the folders you pick, sorted by name or by newest, with a path back up at every step.

  • Search by name

    Type part of a file name and get matches from every shared folder, newest first. On a Mac it uses Spotlight.

  • Preview before you save

    Photos, PDFs, video, audio and text open right in the app. Other files go straight to the share sheet.

  • Save it, send it

    Save to Files, open in Preview, AirDrop it, or attach it to WhatsApp or Mail, from the iPhone share sheet.

  • Works away from home

    Mobile data or any Wi-Fi. While it's plugged in, Remote Desk keeps the laptop awake so it stays reachable.

  • See what was opened

    The laptop keeps a log of every unlock and every file viewed or saved, so nothing happens without you seeing it.

Your files, your phone, nobody else

There's no Remote Desk server and no account with us. Every request has to get past three locks.

  1. Your own network

    Your laptop and phone join your free Tailscale network, which is encrypted end to end. The laptop can't be seen on the Wi-Fi it's on, or on the internet.

  2. One paired phone

    You pair with a one-time code and approve it on the laptop. After that, every other device is turned away, including your own iPad.

  3. A key and a PIN

    Pairing creates a key that stays on that iPhone. Each unlock signs a fresh challenge with it and asks for your PIN; five wrong tries lock it until you unlock it on the laptop.

Never shared, even inside your folders

  • Hidden files like .ssh and .env
  • Keychains and saved browser passwords
  • Password-manager data
  • System folders, Library and AppData
  • Remote Desk's own keys and settings

iCloud Drive: not shared unless you add it, and then the phone asks for your PIN again every time it opens it. You can turn that on for any folder.

Read-only: the phone can open and save copies, but it can never change, move or delete anything on the laptop.

Lost your phone? Click Unpair on the laptop and its key stops working.

Set up once, in about ten minutes

After that, it's just the Home Screen icon and your PIN.

  1. Open Remote Desk on the laptop

    Sign in to Tailscale from its window and turn on HTTPS, which the app walks you through.

  2. Give it access to your files

    On a Mac, switch Remote Desk on in Full Disk Access. Windows needs no extra step.

  3. Add it to your iPhone

    Install the free Tailscale app, scan the QR code on the laptop, then Add to Home Screen.

  4. Pair and choose a PIN

    Type the code from the laptop, pick a 6-digit PIN and click Approve. Done.

Get Remote Desk

There's no signed download yet, so for now you build it yourself on your Mac. It takes a couple of minutes.

You'll need

  • A Mac with Xcode or its Command Line Tools, and Go (brew install go)
  • A free Tailscale account (sign in with Apple, Google, Microsoft or GitHub)
  • An iPhone with the free Tailscale app

Windows: it builds and the code is written for it, but it hasn't been tried on a real PC yet. Android is planned.

# get the code
git clone https://github.com/f00di/remote-desk.git
cd remote-desk

# build the app into ~/Applications
make install

Then open Remote Desk from Applications. The full steps and fixes for common problems are in the README on GitHub.

Questions

Do my files go through someone else's server?

No. They go from your laptop to your phone over your Tailscale network, encrypted end to end. If a direct connection isn't possible, Tailscale relays the encrypted traffic, but it can't read it. There's no Remote Desk server at all.

Do I need iCloud, or an iCloud subscription?

No. Remote Desk gets files straight from your laptop, so you don't have to sync anything to iCloud (or Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox) first, and you don't need to pay for more iCloud storage to reach them. iCloud Drive isn't shared at all unless you add it, and then it asks for your PIN every time.

Does it cost anything?

No, for personal use. Remote Desk is free, Tailscale's personal plan is free, and the phone side is a Home Screen web app, so there's no App Store fee.

Can it change or delete my files?

No. The phone can only list, preview and save copies. Nothing on the laptop can be changed, moved or deleted from the phone.

What if my laptop is asleep?

Then your phone can't reach it. Remote Desk keeps the laptop awake while it's plugged in, but a MacBook still sleeps when you close the lid. On Windows you can set closing the lid to do nothing when it's plugged in.

What if I lose my phone?

Click Unpair on the laptop, and remove the phone from your Tailscale account. Someone holding the phone would also need your PIN, and five wrong tries lock it.

Can I use more than one phone?

Not at the same time. That's on purpose: one laptop works with exactly one phone. To switch, unpair the old one on the laptop and pair the new one.

Android?

Not yet. The phone side is a web app, so Android should mostly work already, but it hasn't been tested.
